Good to know

Bathroom installation FAQs

How much does a new bathroom cost in London?

Most full bathroom renovations range from around £6,000 for a compact bathroom to £20,000+ for larger or high-specification bathrooms. The final price depends on the size of the room, the products and tiles you choose, and whether the layout, plumbing or electrics are being moved. We always provide a clear, itemised fixed quote before any work begins.

How long does it take to fit a bathroom?

A typical bathroom installation takes around 7 to 12 working days from strip-out to finish. Wet rooms, larger bathrooms and jobs involving moving walls or plumbing can take longer. We agree a clear programme before we start and keep you updated daily.

Do you supply the bathroom or do I buy it myself?

Either works. Many clients ask us to design and supply the whole bathroom — suite, tiles, screens, lighting and fittings — at trade prices so everything is coordinated and guaranteed. If you have already bought items, we are happy to fit a supply-only bathroom too.

Are your fitters insured and guaranteed?

Yes. We are fully insured, work is carried out by experienced tradespeople, and every installation is backed by a written workmanship guarantee. Electrical and plumbing work is completed to current UK regulations.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover all of London, Hertfordshire and the Home Counties, including Barnet, Camden, Islington, Westminster, Kensington & Chelsea, St Albans, Watford, Borehamwood, Elstree and the surrounding areas.

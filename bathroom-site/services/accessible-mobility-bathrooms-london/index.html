Overview

Accessible & Mobility Bathrooms in London by one trusted, joined-up team

Accessible bathrooms should feel like a normal, beautiful bathroom — not a hospital. We focus on getting the practical details right (access, safety, ease of use) while keeping the design contemporary and homely.

We work sensitively around individual needs and timescales, and can advise on suitable layouts and fittings for changing mobility. Every installation is completed to current UK regulations and backed by our workmanship guarantee.

Frequently asked questions

Can you convert a bath into a walk-in shower?

Yes. Replacing a bath with a walk-in or level-access shower is one of the most popular and effective accessibility upgrades.

Do you install grab rails and seating?

Yes. We fit grab rails, shower seats, slip-resistant flooring and comfort-height fittings as part of a coordinated design.

Can the bathroom still look stylish?

Definitely. We design accessible bathrooms that are safe and easy to use while looking modern and attractive.

What good MEP looks like on a London project

Riser coordination is the single biggest predictor of a smooth MEP install in London. Central-London plant rooms and risers are tight, structural provision for services is often minimal in period buildings, and multiple services trades sharing a chase without a coordinated model produces the classic on-site clash that costs weeks. We coordinate services in 3D at design stage and mark up as-built deviations as they occur, not at handover.

Commissioning is where good MEP separates itself from cheap MEP. Every zone, every setpoint, every valve, every damper actuator is tested against the design intent; witness testing records are signed off before Practical Completion, and the O&amp;M pack captures the as-built condition so the FM team can maintain it. TR19 duct cleaning, F-Gas leak testing and BS 7671 verification are the minimum, not the optional extras.

Frequently asked questions

Are your electricians NICEIC registered?

Yes. All electrical work is carried out by NICEIC-registered electricians, tested and certified in accordance with BS 7671:2018 Amendment 2. Part P notification is submitted for domestic works under Building Regulations. An Electrical Installation Certificate is issued on completion, and an EICR is provided where the works form part of a refurbishment of an existing installation.

Do you commission to CIBSE Commissioning Codes?

Yes. HVAC commissioning follows the applicable CIBSE Commissioning Codes (A: air distribution, B: boilers, C: automatic controls, R: refrigerating systems, W: water distribution) and BSRIA commissioning guides. Witness testing sheets are issued for each system and included in the handover pack.

Which controls protocol do you use: BACnet, KNX or Modbus?

Depends on the project. BACnet/IP is the pragmatic default for a commercial fit-out with a real BMS front-end. KNX is common on high-spec residential where lighting, blinds, HVAC and multi-room audio need to interoperate. Modbus is used where individual plant items (chillers, VRF outdoor units, meters) expose it natively. A hybrid is not unusual: BACnet backbone with Modbus polling at plant.

How do you handle F-Gas and refrigerant?

All refrigerant handling is by F-Gas certified engineers under the F-Gas Regulation. Systems above the notifiable threshold are leak-tested at the required interval, records are kept for the site and copied into the O&M pack, and end-of-life recovery is documented. Refrigerant selection considers Global Warming Potential and the client's ESG position.

Do you install and certify fire alarms?

Yes. Fire alarm systems are designed and installed to BS 5839-1 for commercial premises and BS 5839-6 for domestic dwellings, in the appropriate category (L1, L2, L3, L4, L5 or M for commercial, LD1 to LD3 for domestic). System commissioning and a Certificate of Design, Installation and Commissioning are issued on completion, and a maintenance contract is offered.

What is included in the O&M handover pack?

As-built drawings, product data sheets, commissioning records for every system, test certificates (BS 7671 EIC, gas certificates, F-Gas records, sprinkler and life-safety), maintenance instructions with recommended intervals, and a demonstration session with the incoming FM or user. Digital and printed copies are provided.
